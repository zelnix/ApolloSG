## App Title
ApolloSG — Scam & Threat Guard

## Short Description (80 characters max)
AI-powered scam detection. Checks links, messages, calls and emails for threats.

## Full Description (4000 characters max)

ApolloSG is your personal security guard for the digital world. It uses AI to check links, messages, phone calls, and emails for scams, phishing, and other threats — before they can harm you.

🛡️ WHAT APOLLOSG DOES

• Website Protection — Filters dangerous websites automatically using a local VPN. No data leaves your device unless a threat is found.

• Link Checking — Paste or share any suspicious link. ApolloSG investigates the domain, checks for known scams, and gives you a clear verdict.

• Message Screening — Share a text message, WhatsApp chat, or email. ApolloSG reads it for red flags like urgency tricks, fake brands, and credential phishing.

• Call Screening — Works with Android's built-in caller ID. Flags suspicious numbers and repeat callers that don't match your contacts.

• Email Monitoring — Connect your mailbox for automatic scanning. ApolloSG checks incoming emails for phishing links and impersonation.

• Government Scam Alerts — Curated alerts from consumer protection agencies in Australia, the US, UK, EU, and globally. Filtered by your region.

• Device Protection Audit — Checks your phone's security settings: screen lock, app permissions, developer options, unknown sources, accessibility services, and more. Flags anything that could put you at risk.

🤖 ASK HIGGINS — YOUR AI SECURITY ASSISTANT

Higgins is ApolloSG's built-in AI investigator. Ask it anything:
• "Is this link safe?"
• "Someone called me from this number — is it real?"
• "I got an email from PayPal asking me to verify my account."

Higgins investigates using multiple sources, checks domain registrations, analyses language patterns, and gives you a plain-English verdict with evidence.

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY ASSIST

Help family members who aren't tech-savvy. Connect to a family member's device to see their threat cases and help them make decisions — with their permission.

📱 DEVICE PROTECTION CHECKS

ApolloSG audits your phone's security posture:
• Screen lock and biometrics
• App permissions (overlay, accessibility, device admin)
• Unknown app sources
• Developer options
• Private DNS configuration
• VPN status

For settings ApolloSG can't read directly, you can send a screenshot and Higgins will verify it for you.
